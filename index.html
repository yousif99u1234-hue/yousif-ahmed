Conjuring I The Timeline Full-stack · 3D · Mobile

An Interactive Museum of Art History

Walk into the painting

Next.js 14 App Router TypeScript React Three Fiber three.js + drei Framer Motion Zustand PostgreSQL Capacitor iOS + Android Playwright Vercel
  • ◆A zoomable, infinite-canvas timeline spanning fifteen periods of art history, from Medieval & Gothic through to Contemporary.
  • ◆Every artist opens into a first-person, walkable 3D gallery hung with their real paintings — PBR materials, per-painting spotlights, soft shadows, a reflective floor and ACES tone mapping.
  • ◆Three switchable timeline designs, including an epoch ribbon that dissolves a period into its individual artists at their true working dates.
  • ◆Built on a live data pipeline drawing from the Wikipedia REST API, Wikidata SPARQL and Wikimedia Commons — every bio, image, date and fact sourced, none of it generated.
  • ◆Packaged with Capacitor into native iOS and Android builds, covered by Playwright, deployed on Vercel.

Conjuring II Wings Product · Brand · Conversion

Wings — Restaurant Brand & Ordering Site
